How to Play PROMPTCRAFT AI

Learn the game in 7 simple steps

1

Look at the Image

A new AI-generated image will be displayed on your screen. Take a moment to observe it carefully.

2

Analyze the Details

Think about what you see: colors, objects, style, mood, and background. What makes this image unique?

3

Guess the Prompt

Try to imagine what text prompt was used to create this AI image. Think like a prompt engineer!

4

Type Your Answer

Type your guessed prompt in the text box. Be descriptive and specific about what you see.

5

Submit Your Guess

Click the "Submit Guess" button and wait for the AI to analyze your answer.

6

See Your Score

Get instant feedback! You'll see a percentage score and which keywords you got right or missed.

7

Try to Reach 90%

To clear the level, you need to reach 90% or higher. You can try multiple times until you succeed!

How Scoring Works

✓ Correct Keywords

Words from the original prompt that match your guess are shown in GREEN.

✗ Missing Keywords

Important words you missed are shown in RED. These help you improve!

51-75%: Close!
76-89%: Very close!
90%+: Level cleared! ✨
